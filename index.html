Remember — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026

Remember is a notes, flashcards and focus-timer app published by Cosmind ("we"). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

The short version

  • Everything you create in Remember stays on your device.
  • There is no account, no sync, no ads and no product analytics.
  • The only data that leaves your device is a crash report, sent when the app crashes.

Data that stays on your device

Your notes, flashcards, decks, tasks, focus sessions, review history and settings are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. We do not receive, see or have any way to access this content.

Remember can export a backup file. That file is written to the location you choose and is never sent to us. Remember does not use Android's automatic cloud backup.

Data we collect: crash reports

When the app crashes, it sends a crash report through Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google. We use these reports only to find and fix bugs.

What is sentThe technical stack trace of the crash, the app version, device model, operating system version, device state at the time of the crash (such as orientation and free memory), the time of the crash, and a randomly generated installation identifier used to count how many installs a crash affects.
What is not sentYour notes, flashcards, tasks or any other content you create. Crash reports contain no app log lines.
Who processes itGoogle, as our service provider for Firebase Crashlytics. We do not sell this data or share it with anyone else.
How long it is keptCrashlytics keeps crash data for 90 days.
In transitCrash reports are encrypted in transit.

Google's handling of this data is described in the Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Notifications — to show the running focus timer, tell you when a session ends, and send an optional daily review reminder.
  • Alarms and foreground service — so a focus session ends at exactly the time you set, even while the app is in the background.
  • Run at startup — to restore reminders after the device restarts.

None of these permissions is used to collect data about you.

Your choices

Because your content never leaves your device, you control it directly: deleting it in the app, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app removes it. To ask about crash data associated with your installation, contact us at the address below.

Children

Remember is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, we will update this page and its effective date before the change ships.
